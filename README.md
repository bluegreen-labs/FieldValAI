Climate change actively threatens the lives and livelihoods of smallholder farmers. Despite
their small scale smallholders produce an estimated 80% of the food in Sub-Saharan Africa
and Asia. It is therefore key to support climate change adaptation in these important
smallholder agriculture systems. However, providing products and services to smallholder
farmers comes with high transaction costs. As a result, they form an underserved market for
data driven instruments to mitigate climate change impacts such as insurance, policy and
management advisories. We aim to bridge this data gap, by developing a large dataset which
combines field scale labelled images of crop development and disturbance status, gathered
over multiple years in the innovative “picture-based insurance” (PBI) project, and remote
sensing and climate variables as data cubes to provide analysis ready multi-modal data for AI
applications. Data will be provided as archived static data, and a dynamic cloud native
SpatioTemporal Asset Catalog (STAC). We hereby make previously unavailable data
available to various stakeholders, for use in policy and commercial products, ranging from
local crop insurance providers to global remote sensing product validation.
